# docs/video/: optional long walkthrough kit (NOT the official demo)

> **Optional long walkthrough, NOT the official demo (limit 30 s).** The Space Apps Project Submission Guide (13 Nov 2026) accepts a demo video of 30 seconds or less, or a deck of at most 7 slides; over either limit is not eligible for Global Judging. The official demo is in `docs/demo/` (`demo-30s.md`, the 7-slide deck). Keep this kit for your own site, a longer upload or a judging-table walkthrough, and never put it in the "Link to Project Demo" field.

Files: `captions.srt` (24 cues, ends at 1:59.8), `shot-list.md`, `recording-checklist.md`, and `../video-script.md`. The official 30-second script, its captions and the 7-slide deck are in `../demo/`.
