# Use of Super Intelligence (SI)

Built with SI assistance (Claude, Anthropic).

This file is the project's SI acknowledgment, as the NASA Space Apps 2026 Project Submission Guide (13 Nov 2026, p. 9) asks. It is factual about what SI was used for. It says nothing about who had the idea or the mission concept: that is for the project owner to state, in field 6 of the submission form (`docs/submission-form.md`).

## What SI was used for

| Category | SI used? | What |
|---|---|---|
| Code | Yes | Claude (Anthropic) wrote most of the game's TypeScript code, the build and audit scripts, and the demo-deck generator. |
| Tests and checks | Yes | Claude wrote the unit and DOM tests and the real-browser audit scripts, and ran them. |
| Text | Yes | Claude wrote the documentation, the in-game text, the demo script and the submission drafts. The owner reviews and edits them. |
| Images and slides | Yes | The demo slides were generated by a script written with Claude. The screenshots are captures of the running game, with a visible "Built with SI assistance (Claude, Anthropic)" strip added. |
| Audio | No | The game has no audio and none was generated. |
| Data | No | No data was generated by SI. The NASA values come from the Moon Fact Sheet snapshot in `src/data/snapshots/moon-facts.json`. |
| NASA logos or branding | No | None was used or generated. |

Claude is the model that did this work. It is not a member of the team and is not listed as one.

## Where the acknowledgment appears

Descriptive text and metadata, as the guide requires for code, text and data:

- README: section "Use of Super Intelligence (SI)".
- This file, `SI_DISCLOSURE.md`, also copied into the offline package.
- The Attributions page (`public/attributions.html`, section `si-disclosure`), plus its `<meta name="si-disclosure">` tag.
- `index.html`: `<meta name="si-disclosure">` and `<meta name="description">`.
- The game footer: the visible line "Built with SI assistance (Claude, Anthropic)", with a link to the Attributions page.
- The demo slides: a footer on every slide, and the title, author and keywords in the `.pptx` and `.pdf` metadata.
- Screenshots in `submission/screenshots-si/`: a visible strip on each image.
- `LICENSE` and `package.json` carry the MIT licence; the SI note is not a licence term.
- Submission form field 6 (`docs/submission-form.md`).

For git commits, a trailer such as `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` marks the commits made with Claude's help.

## What the guide says (so the rules can be checked)

From the Project Submission Guide, 13 Nov 2026, p. 9: SI may be used, but not to come up with the idea of how to solve the challenge; images and videos need a visible watermark; audio, code and data must be acknowledged in descriptive text and in metadata; no NASA logos or branding in generated content. The wording of those rules is in `docs/truth-ledger.md` (E7).

## What only the owner can say

- Who came up with the idea of how to solve the challenge.
- Who came up with the mission concept.
- Whether the owner reviewed and verified each part.

These are left blank on purpose.
