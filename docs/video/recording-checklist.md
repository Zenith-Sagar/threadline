# Recording checklist (one page)

> **Optional long walkthrough, NOT the official demo (limit 30 s).** The Space Apps Project Submission Guide (13 Nov 2026) accepts a demo video of 30 seconds or less, or a deck of at most 7 slides; over either limit is not eligible for Global Judging. The official demo is in `docs/demo/` (`demo-30s.md`, the 7-slide deck). Keep this checklist for your own site, a longer upload or a judging-table walkthrough, and never put it in the "Link to Project Demo" field.


**Before you record**
- [ ] Run `npm run audit:all` (all stages pass) and `npm run screenshots`; open the five images and check they look right.
- [ ] Rehearse once against `docs/video/shot-list.md` with a timer. Aim for 2:00 or less (this long walkthrough only; the official demo is 30 s or less, see `docs/demo/demo-30s.md`).
- [ ] Say the voice-over aloud once: it must not need more than 2.5 words per second in any row (the script was fixed for this).

**Screen**
- [ ] Capture size **1280 x 720** (the size the screenshots use). Browser zoom **100%**. Window not resized during a take.
- [ ] A fresh browser profile or press **Reset progress** twice, so no saved progress or "best result" shows.
- [ ] Hide bookmarks, other tabs, notifications, your name and any private address.
- [ ] Reduced motion **off** (the threads need motion). No sound is needed: the game has no audio.
- [ ] Address: `/?seed=29` for shots 1 to 3 and the same for all shots; or `/?demo=1` for the Demo option (the **Demo** badge shows; that is fine).

**Captions**
- [ ] Use `docs/video/captions.srt` (24 cues, ends at 1:59.8). Check each cue against the voice-over after editing.

**Event requirements (verified 2026-11-14 from the Project Submission Guide and Judging Guide, both dated 13 Nov 2026)**
- [ ] Official demo: video of 30 s or less, or 7 slides or fewer. This 2-minute kit does not qualify; the official demo is `docs/demo/demo-30s.md`.
- [ ] English only (a video in another language needs English subtitles). No image, video, audio or likeness of anyone under 18.
- [ ] Hosted at a public link that needs no login; test it in a private window. Only the form's one demo link is judged as the demo.
- [ ] SI-generated video needs a visible watermark ("Built with SI assistance (Claude, Anthropic)") and the same words in its description text.

**What NOT to claim (say or show)**
- Do not say or imply that NASA made, approved, endorsed or is affiliated with the project. Keep "Independent project, not affiliated with NASA" in the voice-over and on screen.
- Do not call it a simulator or say it is accurate or realistic. It is a game model; only three numbers (the Moon's radius, its gravitational parameter and its distance) come from a NASA page.
- Do not say it was tested on Firefox, Safari, phones or by real players. It was tested in Chromium by script, and no real player has tried it yet.
- Do not say it meets an accessibility standard. Say what was done (keyboard use, contrast checks, a high-contrast theme, text sizes), not a compliance level.
- Do not quote any number that is not on screen. Numbers shown are results of this version of the game.
- Do not describe how the project was written or by whom: the AI-usage wording is pending the event's rule. Leave it to the submission form.
- Do not mention live NASA data, images or logos: there are none.

**After recording**
- [ ] Watch it muted with captions on: does it still make sense?
- [ ] Watch for any visible error, wrong tab, or a number that disagrees with the voice-over.
- [ ] Export, then check the file plays, has sound and captions, and is under the event's limits.
